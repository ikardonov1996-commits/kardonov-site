What we do

Neuro-trainers

We help people control stress, handle anxiety and measure how stress-resilient they are right now, with trainers built on a heart-rate sensor and a neurointerface.

Formats where a person's pulse and state become part of the game.

Neuromafia: Mafia with heart-rate sensors

The rules are familiar: roles, night, the vote. Except now every heartbeat is on the screen. Players persuade, lie under pressure, read reactions and learn to stay calm when the body gives away more than the words.

Mind and Magic: a game where your inner state drives the magic

Sensors read the pulse, and the magic responds to what is happening inside. The calmer a player stays, the more control they get over the water mechanic.

For corporate events, HR sessions, wellness programs, training and neurotech demos.

Reactions to stimuli

We use biosignals to show how people really react to tastes, smells, images and sounds.

For events, PR and advertising we show how the brain and pulse respond to taste, smell, sound and video.

We can also match a scent, a recipe or a piece of content to a specific person.

Neuromarketing without the heavy lab.

FAQ

What is a neuro-trainer?
A game that puts your biosignals (pulse, body reactions) on the screen, so you can train a skill with them: staying calm, reading other people, managing your state under pressure.
How is Neuromafia different from regular team building?
Heart-rate sensors join the conversation. The screen shows how the body reacts to pressure, lies, accusations and attempts to stay calm.
What is an AI system for a team?
A set-up process or tool that automates repetitive work: documents, reports, content, customer replies, knowledge bases.
How is AI training different from a lecture about AI?
We teach on the company's real tasks, not abstractions: how to brief AI, check the result and build it into the work.
How do I start working with KARDONOV?
With a task: an event, an AI process, training, a PR activation or a product idea. Then we work out the scenario, propose the mechanics and agree on the launch format.

Got a task for AI, biosignals or an immersive format?

We'll build a solution you can launch in a team, a training, an event or a product.
